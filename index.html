About Me

A little about the person, curiosity and journey behind Scorpian.

👩‍💻 Who I Am

I am Rakshitha K., a Computer Science and Design student who enjoys exploring the space where technology, creativity and human experiences meet. I am curious about Artificial Intelligence, design and the many ways technology can be transformed into something meaningful and useful.

🔬 What I Explore

I enjoy experimenting with AI, intelligent applications, user experiences, research ideas and creative projects. Rather than learning only from theory, I like turning ideas into experiments and projects while understanding how technology can solve real problems and communicate ideas in a simple and human-friendly way.

🌱 My Journey

I believe every project, experiment, achievement and new skill becomes part of a larger journey. My goal is to keep learning, researching and creating while building my own identity in technology. Scorpian is a reflection of that journey — a place where the things I create and the experiences that shape me can live together.
